Sarabesh

Making a hand-illustrated desert city explorable in the browser.

Sarabesh is the setting for The Last Electrician. The proof of concept lets visitors explore the city through linked panoramas.

Situation

Sarabesh was an illustrated desert city for The Last Electrician.

Task

Make the city feel connected and explorable in the browser.

Action

I created the environment artwork and mapped ten viewpoints and routes, linking them through a chart, first-visit notes and changes in light.

Result

A playable proof of concept with ten panoramic locations, mechanical Dwellers, and lighting that changes with the time of day.

Deliverables

  • An explorable illustrated desert city
  • Ten linked panoramic viewpoints and a navigation chart
  • Day and night scenes with interactive lighting

Connecting the city

I mapped ten viewpoints and nine two-way connections. Doorways and windows show matching parts of the city, so a visitor can recognize where they are going. The linked full-sphere panoramas let people turn in any direction, look into a room, or see the landscape around the landing ground.

A chart that shows what you’ve seen

The illustrated chart uses portholes for the viewpoints and curved routes for their connections. Unvisited places appear in shadow. A first visit opens the location’s journal note, which can be reopened from the chart. The visitor can select another porthole to travel or revisit a discovery.

Lighting as part of the same world

The city has morning, day, evening and night views. Automatic mode follows the visitor’s clock, and the time dial lets them choose a different light. Indoor lights can be switched independently. Coordinating the views through windows keeps the exterior sky consistent with the room and the street.
