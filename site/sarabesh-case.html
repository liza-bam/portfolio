Sarabesh

Making a hand-illustrated desert city explorable in the browser.

Sarabesh is the setting for The Last Electrician. The proof of concept lets visitors explore the city through linked panoramas.

Explore the city
Role
World designer and illustrator
Scope
Environment artwork, panorama routes, map controls, and day-to-night lighting
Timeline
2026 · Browser prototype
What I did
I created the original environment artwork and mapped the ten viewpoints and routes that connect them.

Result

A playable proof of concept with ten panoramic locations, mechanical Dwellers, and lighting that changes with the time of day.
