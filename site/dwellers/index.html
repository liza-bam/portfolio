Three ways to play

Creator

For makers who can’t leave a box of spare parts alone. Build a creature with a character of its own, then set it free.

Hunter

For explorers with an eye for a good photograph. Follow the map, find a Dweller, and bring back a picture worth sharing.

Spectator

For those who enjoy watching a world unfold. Browse the discoveries, leave a comment, and cheer on your favorite creatures.

Why steampunk?

I love old things: the weight of metal, the marks of use, the sense that every object has a history.

Steampunk brings that feeling into the game. A spring becomes an antenna; a clock becomes a belly. Building a Dweller is a small creative project you can share with someone else.

How to play

  1. 1. Create & release

    Build a Dweller from parts and release it at a real-world location.

  2. 2. Find

    Walk nearby. The map shows Dwellers waiting to be discovered.

  3. 3. Photograph

    Use the AR camera to photograph a Dweller, then share it on your feed.

  4. 4. Connect

    Explore other players’ photographs. Leave comments and likes.
