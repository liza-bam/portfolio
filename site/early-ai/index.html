Early AI work

These illustration projects date from 2022–2023, when I began working with AI image tools. I developed the concepts, edited the generated images in Photoshop, and adapted the artwork for china, merchandise, book covers and an interactive panorama tour.

2022–2023

InkPromptu

China, prints and my own store

I developed the name, logo and marketing with ChatGPT. I made the cup designs in Midjourney 4.5 and edited them in Photoshop.

About 250 sales in two months.

The margin was roughly $5 per cup, and the workflow was entirely manual. The return did not justify the time involved. I’m considering reopening the store with Shopify and a more automated process.

I designed four cup-and-saucer sets around California’s seasonal landscapes.

2022–2023

New York, as characters

A self-initiated cover concept for The City We Became by N. K. Jemisin

I asked ChatGPT to describe the gods of New York’s boroughs and neighbourhoods, edited those descriptions into prompts, and developed the characters in Midjourney 5, with final edits in Photoshop.
