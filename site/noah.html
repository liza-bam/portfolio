Noah Fixes the Rainbow

Keeping a child, his monsters, and their watercolor world consistent across 32 pages.

Dmitry Blinder and Sandy Tayts

The main challenge was consistency: the same Noah, the same monsters, and the same watercolor world from page to page.

Role
Illustrator and book designer
Scope
Character development, watercolor illustrations, and page spreads
Timeline
2026 · Portfolio case study
What I did
I explored concepts in Midjourney and combined the images in Photoshop. Then I defined the style in ChatGPT and directed the final illustrations.

Tools

  • Midjourney
  • Adobe Photoshop
  • ChatGPT

Situation

A children’s book needed Noah and the monsters to feel like the same characters from page to page.

Task

Establish a watercolor style that could hold across the whole 32-page book.

Action

I developed Noah’s look and monster character sheets, composited early concepts in Photoshop, and directed the final illustrations and spreads.

Result

A fully illustrated 32-page picture book, with character studies and finished spreads.

Deliverables

  • A finished 32-page illustrated book
  • Character sheets for Noah, Papa and the monsters
  • Designed page spreads
