Situation

Initial state of student retention

The startup wanted to use campus rewards to support student retention, with faculty, students, parents and local businesses all taking part.

Every year more than 20 million full-time students enroll in US colleges, and two out of three do not graduate. LoopedIn, a 2015 startup, wanted to change that through what students do on campus: the faculty pick the habits they want to encourage, such as going to events or eating well, and students earn rewards for them. Parents, faculty, students and local vendors all had a part in it.

Task

What will a good college loyalty portal look like?

Design six everyday student journeys as one app, with a shared UI that could carry each university’s branding.

The task was to create a college loyalty portal: a student app that rewards the habits the faculty choose, with rewards from the school and from local businesses. The portal had to cover six things students do every day: pay, send money, find events, get rewards, buy books and find places to eat. Each college that bought it had to see its own brand, and the features built by different teams had to work as one app.

One app had to do six jobs a student does every day: pay, send money, find events, get rewards, buy books, find places to eat. And it had to look like each school that bought it, not like a vendor.

Action

Design the loyalty portal from discovery to the UI system

I mapped who offered and earned rewards, then made them visible where students were already looking. The calendar marks rewarded events, the store shows what earns points, and the map shows where to redeem them. I designed university-specific skins around a shared icon system. The original work was done in Illustrator and later rebuilt as a prototype with ChatGPT.

In discovery I mapped the school’s goals against what students want and placed the four groups in one loop. I drew the main journeys so each team could see where its features met, and a user flow for acceptance testing. In the UI, rewards ran through every feature: the calendar marks rewarded events, the store shows what earns points, the map shows where a reward is waiting. For schools I designed a skin system with chosen widgets, menu, colors and graphics, a custom icon font and color presets.

Rewards inside every feature

Rewards ran through every feature instead of living on their own tab. The calendar marks rewarded events, the campus store tells you what earns points, and the map shows where your free drink is waiting. The school can promote exactly the events it needs students to attend.

A skin system for each college

Each college chooses its widgets, menu, colors and main graphics. We made a custom icon font for the shared actions and the school-specific ones, and color presets so a school picks a set instead of designing one.

Result

The college loyalty portal reached the market

The app launched as the first of its kind on the market. 150 screens were designed and built in one month, and four universities were onboarded, each with its own skin.
