EY Digital Hub

Designed an omnichannel IT support experience for EY staff. Discovery included stakeholder workshops, surveys, interviews, and past incident reviews. The work led to a five-year implementation proposal with projected improvements in support costs and employee productivity.

Lazy

Designed a no-code website builder for independent vacation rental hosts. It uses listing photos and property details to start the site. Hosts can update the design and content in an editor and choose how guests book. The case study covers host research, the service offer, the design system, and the editor.

Dwellers

Designed a steampunk scavenger hunt with three ways to play. Creators assemble and release creatures, hunters find and photograph them, and spectators browse discoveries. The project includes a playable prototype.

No Grounds for Panic

Co-wrote this illustrated science-fiction collection with Alex Kartashov. Created the black-and-white illustrations with Midjourney and refined them in Photoshop. The project page has selected illustrations and a book viewer.

Early AI work

Developed illustration projects in 2022–2023 using AI image tools and Photoshop. The work spans InkPromptu china, botanical dragons, New York characters, and an interactive panorama. The InkPromptu shop recorded about 250 sales in two months.
