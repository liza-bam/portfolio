Open WebsitePleasant Street Use Case
Role
Founder and Product Designer
Scope
Create an affordable direct-booking website service that hosts can manage without technical skills.
Timeline
4 months · 2026

Situation

Airbnb got worse for hosts: higher fees, new rules, picky reviews.

Business Findings

Hosts depend on Airbnb to get booked, and they pay for it. So they host defensively instead of running a business.

Most hosts who go direct already have a site. Their problem is getting anyone to book on it.

Experience Findings

The blocker is trust, not price.

Task

Hosts want a website they can edit and can’t break.

User research

I’m a host too. Before I designed anything, I went to 15 Facebook groups and asked. 60 posts, 200+ answers. Everybody wanted direct bookings. Nobody wanted to deal with tech, and they were afraid guests would not trust their own site.

I ranked what hosts want from surveys and research, then checked every item against what I was building.

Competitors

A designer costs $500 to $5,000 and then leaves. Rental platforms and Houfy give the host a booking site to build alone. Generic and AI builders know nothing about rentals.

Action

A static website, no vibe-coding, built in four months.

Process

A host sends a link. Lazy builds the website. The host edits it. Guests book.

Architecture

Editing and the live website are two separate things. The editor is an app. A published website is plain files, so it stays up and stays fast whatever happens in the editor.

Wireframes

Then the pages. Two Masthead designs, Hero and Split, as wireframes. Then a whole site in grey boxes: Home, Guidebook, Your stay. No colors until the structure worked.

The editor

I wanted hosts to edit their own website. So before designing pages, I built a rough editor next to a real page. It works. The host picks a Masthead design, a Skin, fonts and colors from options I designed. Nothing to drag, nothing to break.

Page design

A lake cabin and a Miami condo should not look alike. So the same sections come in seven Masthead designs, and a Skin changes fonts and colors in one click. The words and photos stay put.

This was the hosts’ biggest fear. So reviews go near the top, next to the Trust band, and the host picks which reviews show.

A guest opens the site looking for one thing. The Rail and the Jump strip take them straight there. Points of interest and Weather cover what’s around.

Hosts write a lot or a little, and their photos are whatever they have. Every section has a fixed layout, so it holds up either way, on a phone, a tablet and a computer.

Demos and testing

To test it, I built fifteen very different homes on the same engine. If Kawaii and Tahoe Cabin both work, a real host’s home will.

Marketing website

Then hosts had to hear about it. So I drew a cat and gave the ads a radio voice. The marketing website has its own design system, separate from the host websites.

Result

Ten clients in five weeks. Launched October 12, 2026.
