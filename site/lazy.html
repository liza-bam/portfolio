Situation

Airbnb got worse for hosts: higher fees, new rules, picky reviews.

Business Findings

I’m a host. Airbnb raised my fee from about 3% to 15.5%. Then it changed the rules again. And guests ask for refunds because the bed was tucked in.

Experience Findings

Task

Hosts want a website they can edit and can’t break.

See moreSee less

Client expectations

So I asked other hosts. 60 posts in 15 Facebook groups, 200+ answers. They all wanted direct bookings. They were afraid guests would not trust the site, and that it was too much tech.

Action

A static website, no vibe-coding, built in four months.

Facebook pageSee moreSee less

Process

So I built Lazy. A host sends their Airbnb link, I build the website, they edit it, guests book.

Architecture

No vibe-coding. The published website is plain files, separate from the editor. It can’t break and it’s fast.

Result

Ten clients in five weeks. Launched October 12, 2026.
