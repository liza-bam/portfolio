What I did
I designed how a seller moves from the account overview into a deal, reviews stakeholders and risks, and acts on a suggested next step.

Situation

A sales rep needs to understand what changed in an account and decide what to do about it.

Task

Bring deal history, people and recommended moves into the same workspace.

Action

I designed the account and deal views, the assistant conversation, and the flow from reviewing a risk to taking a suggested action.

Result

The interactive demo shows how a sales team can review deal context and get recommended next steps.

From accounts to individual deals

I designed separate account and deal views so a seller can scan the pipeline, then narrow the interface to a single opportunity. The account view brings deal cards and recommended Moves together. The deal view keeps the company rail, stakeholder cards, recent signals and follow-ups in the same workspace so the seller can keep their place as they move through it.

Showing the reason for a next step

Stakeholder cards and signal history give the user context for a recommendation. I designed that context alongside the action, where it can inform the decision to follow up. Email and meeting editors gather the goal and attachments so the seller can take the next step without reconstructing the deal history in another screen.

The assistant in the workspace

The conversation sits beside the information the rep is working with. Its tables and charts are tied to demo records. On smaller screens the rep can switch between the panels. The browser demo uses scripted responses and rehearsed calls to demonstrate the workflow.
