Privacy

What this site collects, and what it doesn’t.

This is my personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. I don’t collect personal information unless you send it to me.

Contact form and email

The contact form doesn’t send anything through this site. It opens your own email app with your message ready, and nothing goes out until you press Send there. I use what you write only to reply to you. I don’t share it and I don’t add you to any mailing list.

Chat assistant

This site has a chat assistant run by Elfsight. When you use it, your questions and its answers are processed by Elfsight under Elfsight’s privacy policy, and it may store data in your browser. Please don’t enter sensitive personal information in the chat.

Fonts and hosting

Pages use Google Fonts, so your browser requests font files from Google, which receives your IP address under Google’s privacy policy. The site is hosted on Amazon Web Services, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address, page requested, browser and time.

Your browser

The homepage remembers, for your current visit only, that you’ve seen the opening animation so it doesn’t play again. Some prototypes, such as Looped In, save your progress in your browser so you can pick up where you left off. This stays on your device and is never sent to me.

Other sites

Links to LinkedIn, WhatsApp and project websites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Questions

To ask what I have about you, or to have it deleted, write to sandy@tayts.com.
