# BP case study — what Sandy did, by stage, with role per step

Saved November 25, 2026, for the role pills in the online case study (https://sandy.tayts.com/bigoil.html). Roles: UX and service designer; DocuSign delivery lead. Steps marked "?" have no role assigned yet.

## 1. Discovery
- Found champions in the markets — UX and service designer
- Ran workshops over several weeks — UX and service designer
- Mapped the as-is processes — UX and service designer
- Designed the to-be processes and agreed them with stakeholders — UX and service designer

## 2. Three platforms
- Worked through the diagrams with the architects and leads of Salesforce, Automation and DocuSign — UX and service designer
- Designed how users move between platforms — UX and service designer
- Shaped the team around a design-first approach — UX and service designer

## 3. Contracts
- Collected and processed all contracts — ?
- Designed the modular template structure — ? (both design and a solution for the DocuSign team)
- Designed the move from Salesforce to DocuSign and the DocuSign flows — DocuSign delivery lead
- Ran daily stand-ups and set the team's tasks — DocuSign delivery lead
- Trained the junior developers — DocuSign delivery lead

## 4. Launch
- Recorded a video and a cheat sheet for every flow — ?
- Came up with the Center of Excellence and led 3 analysts — UX and service designer
- Wrote test plans and organized asynchronous testing — DocuSign delivery lead
- Ran demos for the whole team (the full sales process) — UX and service designer
